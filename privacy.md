Privacy Policy for Gardenio
Last updated: September 9, 2026

Gardenio is a lawn care application operated by VOCHIȚA CĂTĂLIN-CONSTANTIN PERSOANĂ FIZICĂ AUTORIZATĂ, registered in Romania under registration number F2026015743000, CUI 54325346 (“Gardenio”, “we”, “us”).

This Privacy Policy explains what information Gardenio processes, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

Gardenio may process information you provide when setting up and using the application, including information about your lawn and its characteristics, such as lawn size, soil type, sun exposure and other lawn-care preferences.

Gardenio may also store activities you record in the application, such as watering, mowing, fertilizing, aerating, scarifying and other lawn-care activities.

Location and weather information

If you grant location permission, Gardenio may use your location to obtain local weather information and provide location-specific lawn-care recommendations.

Location information is used for functionality such as weather forecasts and personalized recommendations. Gardenio does not use location information for advertising.

You may deny or revoke location permission through your device settings.

Weather services

Gardenio may communicate with third-party weather and geocoding services, including Open-Meteo, to obtain weather and location-related information necessary for the application's functionality.

Information sent to these services is limited to what is necessary to provide the requested functionality and is also subject to the applicable service provider's privacy practices.

Information stored on your device

Gardenio stores certain information locally on your device, including lawn profile information, application preferences and lawn-care activity history.

Removing the application may result in deletion of locally stored information unless a backup or synchronization feature is introduced and explicitly communicated to you.

Notifications

If you grant permission, Gardenio may send notifications relating to lawn-care activities and recommendations.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

Subscriptions and payments

Gardenio may offer optional Premium subscriptions.

Purchases made through the iOS application are processed by Apple through the App Store. Gardenio does not directly receive or store your complete payment card details.

Apple's processing of payment information is governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

How we use information

We process information only as necessary to:

provide and operate Gardenio;
generate lawn-care recommendations;
provide weather-based functionality;
maintain your lawn-care history and preferences;
provide Premium functionality;
respond to support requests;
maintain and improve the reliability of the application; and
comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data sharing

Gardenio does not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to service providers where necessary to operate specific application functionality, such as weather services or Apple's App Store infrastructure.

Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by Gardenio remains on your device until it is deleted through available application functionality, the application's data is cleared, or the application is removed, subject to device backup functionality.

If future versions of Gardenio introduce user accounts or cloud synchronization, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

You may contact us regarding privacy or deletion requests at the email address below.

Children

Gardenio is not designed specifically for children and is intended as a lawn-care utility application.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling purposes.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Gardenio. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Gardenio evolves. The current version will always be made available through the application's Privacy Policy URL.
