Gardenio Support

Need help with Gardenio?

For questions, technical issues, Premium subscription problems, or feedback, contact us at:

support.gardenio@gmail.com

When reporting a technical issue, please include your device model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. Please do not send passwords or payment card information.

Subscriptions

Gardenio Premium subscriptions purchased through the App Store can be managed from your Apple account subscription settings.

If you previously purchased Gardenio Premium and it is not recognized by the app, use Restore Purchases inside Gardenio.

Billing and refund requests for App Store purchases may be subject to Apple's applicable procedures.
